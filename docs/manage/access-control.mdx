# Access control

Access in WSO2 Cloud works at two levels:

- **Organization roles** decide what a user can do across the organization, such as managing users and service contacts.
- **Service roles** decide what a user can do inside each service, such as building or deploying agents. Each service defines and manages its own roles.

## Organization roles

Every user in an organization has one organization role. You choose it when you [invite the user](./invite-users.mdx).

| Role | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **OrganizationAdmin** | People who administer the organization: its users, its service contacts, and every service. |
| **Member** | Everyone else in the organization. |

An **OrganizationAdmin** is also an admin in every service. Give this role only to the people who need to administer the organization.

## Service roles

Each service ships its own predefined roles, shaped around the jobs people do in that service. For example, Agent Management has **Admin**, **Developer**, **AI Lead**, and **Platform Engineer** roles. To learn about a service's roles, see its documentation:

- [Agent Management](https://wso2.com/agent-platform/docs/cloud/concepts/organization/)
