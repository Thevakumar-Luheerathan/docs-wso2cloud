# Manage your subscription

This article shows you how to make sure the right people receive your invoices, and how to get help with subscription changes. To learn how plans and billing work, see [Subscriptions and billing](./subscriptions-and-billing.mdx).

## Prerequisites

- The **OrganizationAdmin** role in the organization.
- Your organization's Organization Unit ID. See [View organization details](./view-organization-details.mdx).

## Set who receives invoices

1. Sign in to the [WSO2 Cloud console](https://console.cloud.wso2.com).
2. Go to **Admin** > **Service Contacts**, and select the **Billing** tab.
3. Add or edit contacts so the right people receive invoices, payment issues, and subscription notices.

For details, see [Manage service contacts](./manage-service-contacts.mdx).

## Subscribe to the Enterprise tier

Every service starts on the **Free** tier, a 30-day free trial. To keep using a service after the trial, subscribe to its **Enterprise** tier through AWS Marketplace. See [Subscriptions and billing](./subscriptions-and-billing.mdx#subscriptions).

## Change your subscription or ask about a bill

Contact [WSO2 support](../support.mdx). Include your Organization Unit ID, the service, and what you need.
