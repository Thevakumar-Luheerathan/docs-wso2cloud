# Invite users

This article shows you how to invite people to your organization. You invite each person by email and choose their organization role.

## Prerequisites

- The **OrganizationAdmin** role in the organization.
- The email address each person will sign in with. It must be the email address of their Google or GitHub account.

## Invite a user

1. Sign in to the [WSO2 Cloud console](https://console.cloud.wso2.com).
2. In the sidebar, under **Admin**, select **Users**.
3. Select **Invite user**.
4. In **Email address**, enter the person's email address.
5. In **Role**, select **Member** or **OrganizationAdmin**. To choose, see [Access control](./access-control.mdx#organization-roles).
6. Select **Send invitation**.

The person receives an invitation email. To join, they accept the invitation and sign in with the exact email address you entered. They then join your organization with the role you selected. That role decides what they can do across the organization. Inside each service, service roles decide what they can do. See [Access control](./access-control.mdx).

## Find a user

1. In the sidebar, under **Admin**, select **Users**.
2. Use **Search users** to find a user.

The list shows each user's **Name**, **Email**, and **Organization Unit**.
