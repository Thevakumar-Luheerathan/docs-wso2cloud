# Subscriptions and billing

Billing works the same way for every service in your organization. Each service has its own subscription plan and is billed separately. WSO2 sends invoices and billing notices to your organization's billing contacts.

## Subscriptions

A subscription plan decides which features and limits apply to your organization in a service. Every service offers two tiers:

| Tier | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Free** | A free trial for 30 days. |
| **Enterprise** | A paid subscription, which you buy through AWS Marketplace. |

Features and limits in each tier differ by service. See each service's documentation for details.

## Billing contacts

Billing contacts receive invoices, payment issues, and notices about subscription changes. Your organization always has at least one billing contact. To choose who they are, see [Manage service contacts](./manage-service-contacts.mdx).
