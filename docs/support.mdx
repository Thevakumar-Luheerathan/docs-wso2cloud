# Get support

This article shows you how to get help with WSO2 Cloud, and which support plan gives you which kind of help.

## Prerequisites

- To open a support ticket, a Basic or Enterprise support plan.
- Your Organization Unit ID. See [View organization details](./manage/view-organization-details.mdx).

## Choose a support channel

| Plan | Channel | Availability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Community** | [WSO2 Discord](https://discord.com/invite/wso2) | Community help |
| **Basic** | [WSO2 support portal](https://support.wso2.com/support) | 12x5 |
| **Enterprise** | [WSO2 support portal](https://support.wso2.com/support) | 24x7x365 |

Enterprise support is purchased separately. For response times by severity, contact [cloud@wso2.com](mailto:cloud@wso2.com).

## Check the documentation first

- Check the [frequently asked questions](./reference/faq.mdx).
- Check the documentation of the service you're using. Each service lists its own troubleshooting steps.

## Open a support ticket

1. Sign in to the [WSO2 support portal](https://support.wso2.com/support). You can also select **Support** at the bottom of the WSO2 Cloud console.
2. Create a ticket, and choose the severity that matches the impact.
3. Include your Organization Unit ID and the service the issue is in.
