# Frequently asked questions

## Accounts and organizations

### What is the difference between my account and my organization?

Your account is you, the person who signs in. Your organization is the tenant that holds users, service contacts, and resources. See [Key concepts](../get-started/key-concepts.mdx).

### Can I belong to more than one organization?

You can create one organization for now. You can join other organizations when their administrators invite you. See [Organizations](../manage/organizations.mdx#one-organization-per-user).

### How do I sign in?

With your Google or GitHub account. See [Sign up and create your organization](../get-started/sign-up.mdx).

### Where are my projects, APIs, and agents?

Projects are shared across services, so you see the same projects in every service except Platform Engineering, which keeps its own. Components, such as agents, integrations, and APIs, are managed in the service you built them with. See [Key concepts](../get-started/key-concepts.mdx#projects) and [Services](../get-started/what-is-wso2-cloud.mdx#services).

## Users and access

### How do I add people to my organization?

Invite them by email and choose their organization role. See [Invite users](../manage/invite-users.mdx).

### Who does WSO2 notify about maintenance, security, and billing?

Your organization's service contacts. See [Manage service contacts](../manage/manage-service-contacts.mdx).

## Billing

### Is there a free plan?

Every service has a **Free** tier, a 30-day free trial. After the trial, subscribe to the **Enterprise** tier through AWS Marketplace. See [Subscriptions and billing](../manage/subscriptions-and-billing.mdx).

### Who do I contact about my bill?

Contact [WSO2 support](../support.mdx). To choose who receives invoices, see [Manage your subscription](../manage/manage-your-subscription.mdx).
